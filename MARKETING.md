# MARKETING — Plan for Pinyn (Pinyin Corrector)

Status: pre-launch (Chrome Web Store). Monetization is donation-based; paid
tiers come later (see MONETIZATION.md). This document is the prioritized
launch funnel — from cheapest/highest ROI to later, paid experiments.

## 1. Honest expectations

- The Chrome Web Store is already a marketplace: store search + reviews drive
  a share of discovery **for free**. Good listing = the first channel.
- The niche audience (Chinese learners HSK 1–9) mostly lives **outside** the
  store: Reddit, YouTube/TikTok, forums, universities.
- Current LTV is low (donations). **Paid ads do not pay back until a paid tier
  exists** (LTV > CAC). Today: waste of money.
- Reviews on the store are the critical asset: without the first ~10–20,
  Chrome won't push the listing. Early marketing = seeding reviews, not volume.

## 2. The funnel (priority order)

1. ASO — free, do before/at launch.
2. Niche communities — organic, high relevance.
3. Seed reviews — algorithm boost.
4. Educational content — product as the topic.
5. Paid experiments — only once a paid tier justifies the CAC.

## 3. ASO checklist (pre-launch)

- [ ] **English listing.** The current store description is Italian; for a
  global niche of Chinese learners the description and keywords must be
  English. Cost zero, biggest single lever.
- [ ] Suggested manifest description (≤132 chars):
  `Contextual pinyin correction and Chinese-character conversion for Chinese learners (HSK 1-9).`
- [ ] Keywords to weave in: `pinyin`, `Chinese`, `HSK`, `tone marks`,
  `typing`, `hanzi`, `dictionary`.
- [ ] Category: Education (or Productivity) — pick the one with best
  discoverability for language tools.
- [ ] Screenshots (1280×800 / 640×400) showing the visible flow: red underline
  on a wrong tone + the 1–9 suggestion menu + pinyin→hanzi conversion.
- [ ] Long description: target (HSK 1–9 learners) + core features + 100% local
  privacy + free/open-source + support link.

## 4. Niche channels

- **Reddit** — r/ChineseLanguage, r/HSK, r/languagelearning: post *useful*
  content ("how to type pinyin with correct tones"), not self-promo spam
  (self-promo rules can get you banned). Answer questions and link when relevant.
- **Chinese-learning Discords / forums / WeChat groups** — join, help, mention.
- **Universities with Chinese programs** — direct relevance; aligns with the
  B2B school-build plan (see MONETIZATION.md).

## 5. Demo content (YouTube / TikTok / Shorts)

The product is visually strong: a wrong tone underlined in red → live
correction → pinyin becomes hanzi. One 30–60s demo.

- Hook: "Typing pinyin wrong? Pinyn fixes your tones as you type."
- Show: type `ni hao` → accents appear; type a typo → red underline + menu 1–9;
  accept with a number → hanzi inserted.
- Hashtags: #chinese #learnchinese #hsk #pinyin #chineselanguage #languagelearning
- CTA: link to the store page.

## 6. Seed reviews

Invite the first users (community, friends, university students) to leave a
review. The first ~10–20 reviews matter most for Chrome's ranking.

## 7. Educational content

One evergreen post/article: "How to type Chinese with correct tones (and why
you keep mistyping it)". Natural link to the extension. Reusable across
Reddit, a blog, and YouTube description.

## 8. What NOT to do

- Paid ads before a paid tier exists.
- Generic marketing (audience is specific; generic = wasted effort).
- Self-promo spam in subreddits (bannable).
- Neglecting the store listing — it is the primary channel.

## 9. Later — only when a paid tier exists

- Paid ads (Google/YouTube) once LTV > CAC.
- ProductHunt launch, AlternativeTo listing.
- Collabs with Chinese-language YouTube/TikTok channels.
- Cross-promotion with related extensions and study tools.
